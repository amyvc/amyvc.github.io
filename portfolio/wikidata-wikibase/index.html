04 · Product Strategy

Shaping Community-Driven Products

Wikimedia Deutschland e. V.

+20%User satisfaction

The Challenge

Developing globally used open-data products, Wikidata and Wikibase, required balancing diverse community needs, complex technical requirements, and product priorities while improving existing data tools and workflows.

My Role

As IT Product Manager, I supported product strategy and development by translating community requirements, user research, and analytical insights into actionable technical requirements and process improvements.

What I Did

  • Analyzed product performance and user behavior to inform product strategy.
  • Conducted research to identify pain points in internal data tools and data entry workflows.
  • Translated stakeholder requirements into user stories with detailed acceptance criteria in Phabricator.
  • Documented existing business processes using BPMN standards.
  • Contributed to technical initiatives focused on Wikidata constraint validation and item quality evaluation..

Results

  • Increased user satisfaction by 20%.
  • Optimized data entry processes through user research and workflow improvements.
  • Improved requirements clarity through structured user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Supported data-driven product strategy and roadmap alignment.
