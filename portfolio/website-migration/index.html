02 · Enterprise Delivery

Website Migration

Interone – Omnicom

300+Websites
8+On time releases

The Challenge

A large-scale digital platform migration involving 300+ websites across Germany and Switzerland required coordinated technical implementation, platform alignment, and release planning while maintaining existing production operations.

My Role

Led end-to-end migration planning and delivery, coordinating multidisciplinary teams, managing technical dependencies, and aligning implementation priorities with stakeholder requirements.

What I Did

  • Planned and coordinated website migrations across OpenCMS and Drupal environments.
  • Managed technical dependencies, engineering resources, and release sequencing.
  • Coordinated development, UX/UI, QA, infrastructure, content, analytics, and SEO stakeholders.
  • Aligned component development and design requirements with engineering teams.
  • Coordinated analytics tracking, consent management, and digital accessibility requirements.
  • Managed go-live activities, stakeholder communication, and post-launch support.

Results

  • Managed migration delivery for 300+ websites across Germany and Switzerland.
  • Successfully managed onboarding and offboarding of 5+ developers within one year, maintaining uninterrupted delivery with zero release delays.
  • Supported production readiness and operational continuity throughout the migration lifecycle.
