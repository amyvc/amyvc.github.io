Recovering a High-Risk Technical Project

Interone – Omnicom

2×Sprint velocity
12+Markets

The Challenge

A high-risk technical delivery project faced stalled sprint execution, unresolved infrastructure blockers, an overgrown backlog, and recurring weekly client escalations.

My Role

As Technical Project Manager, I stepped in to stabilize delivery operations, align engineering and stakeholder teams, and restore release predictability.

What I Did

  • Restructured sprint ceremonies and introduced backlog readiness standards.
  • Resolved critical infrastructure blockers and cross-team dependencies.
  • Refined and prioritized the backlog, removing outdated requirements.
  • Established structured delivery reporting and clear escalation practices.
  • Protected engineering focus time across UX/UI, QA, DevOps, and development streams.

Results

  • Stabilized the high-risk delivery stream within approximately three weeks.
  • Doubled sprint velocity from 40 to 80 story points, sustaining the improvement for five months.
  • Coordinated international platform delivery across 13 European markets
  • Coordinated 10 consecutive on-schedule production releases.
  • Eliminated recurring weekly client escalations.
