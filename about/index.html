About me

Understanding systems. Connecting people.

I have always been curious about how things work, not just technology, but people, systems, and the connections between them.

Living and working across different cultures and professional environments has shaped how I approach challenges and leadership. It taught me to embrace uncertainty, value diverse perspectives, and find growth in unfamiliar territory.

Where technology meets people

My career has evolved from hands-on engineering into technical project management, bringing together analytical rigor, technical fluency, and human connection.

Across my career, I have worked with cross-functional teams to deliver complex digital initiatives, bridging technical requirements and business objectives.

What motivates me most isn’t just delivering technology, but understanding the problems it solves and the people it serves.

My approach to leadership

To me, good leadership means creating clarity without oversimplifying, building trust without micromanaging, and enabling teams to do their best work.

Meaningful progress happens when people understand not only what they are building, but why it matters.

What drives me

Clarity — Making complex systems and goals understandable.

Execution — Turning ambitious ideas into reliable outcomes.

Foresight — Anticipating risks and long-term implications.

Empathy — Leading with context and genuine perspective.

Growth — Staying curious and adaptable as industries evolve.

Learning isn’t a destination for me; it’s how I move through life.
